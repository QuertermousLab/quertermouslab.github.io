Led by Daniel Li, this study builds a timecourse single-cell transcriptomic and epigenomic map of
smooth muscle cells in a mouse model of atherosclerosis, pairs it with Xenium spatial transcriptomics,
and uses network-based prioritization and in silico transcription-factor perturbation to identify
the regulators — including TCF21 and TEAD1 — that drive SMC cell-state changes. Integration with
CAD GWAS shows that heritable risk concentrates in transitional SMC states.

Data are available on [CELLxGENE](https://cellxgene.cziscience.com/collections/7a3044e4-6b16-4693-9504-212d9a573f80) and GEO (GSE321762).
